How It Works
A coin is launched by sending a payment on X Money. Backers fund it the same way, and the tokens reach them automatically.
STEP 1
Prepare Your Coin
Upload a logo, pick a name and a symbol. You get one metadata link to paste.
STEP 2
Pay On X Money
Send the creation fee to our handle with that config as the note. No wallet, no pop-ups.
STEP 3
The Raise Opens
Your coin appears under Raising. Backers send X Money until the target is met or time runs out.
STEP 4
Launch And Distribute
The coin is created on Pump with an atomic buy, then tokens go out to backers by arrival order.
